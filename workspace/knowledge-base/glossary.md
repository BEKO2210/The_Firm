# Glossary — Korynth Labs

> Domain + firm-specific terminology. Append-only; deprecated terms get a strike-through note.

## A

- **Account Manager** — Communicator archetype role, primary customer interface for Med+/Large/XL tickets.
- **ADR** — Architecture Decision Record. Documented in repo + linked in `workspace/knowledge-base/decisions.md`.
- **Adaptive (pricing/sprint/touchpoints)** — Firm autonomously selects mode per defined rules in CLAUDE.md.
- **Anti-Pattern** — One of 10 toxic behaviors explicitly forbidden in §39 of CLAUDE.md.
- **Archetype** — One of 13 universal role types (Leader, Strategist, Coordinator, Maker, Reviewer, Guardian, Mentor, Communicator, Caretaker, Recorder, Critic, Operator, Releaser).
- **AVV** — Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (data processing agreement per DSGVO Art. 28).

## B

- **Bezos Type-1 / Type-2** — Decision classification: Type-1 = one-way door (irreversible), Type-2 = reversible.
- **Burnout Score** — Numeric score 0-100 per employee; ≥ 80 = forced rest.

## C

- **Critic** — Archetype performing devil's-advocate / adversarial review (§11).
- **Customer co-ownership** — Equity model where long-term high-NPS customers can convert fees to equity (§35.4).

## D

- **DPIA** — Data Protection Impact Assessment per DSGVO Art. 35.
- **DSGVO** — Datenschutz-Grundverordnung (EU GDPR).
- **Drift Alert** — Production-hash mismatch detected at session start (§13).

## G

- **Gate A / Gate B** — Quality gates between Sandbox→Staging (A1–A6) and Staging→Production (B1–B6) per §12.

## H

- **HiPPO** — Highest Paid Person's Opinion. Anti-pattern A.6 — forbidden in decision logs.
- **Human-in-Loop** — Required principal approval before execution of critical AI decisions (§31.2).

## K

- **Knowledge Graph** — Structured knowledge store at `workspace/knowledge-graph/` with auto-decay (§37).

## M

- **Maker** — Archetype producing primary firm output (code, designs, documents, plans).
- **Maximum-care** — Vacation policy: unlimited + 4-week minimum + sabbaticals + mental-health days + forced rest.

## N

- **NPS** — Net Promoter Score (-100 to +100), collected after every delivery (§23).

## P

- **Principal** — The one human the firm reports to. For Korynth Labs: Belkis Aslani.
- **Pool** — Personal workspace + reference per employee at `pools/<id>-<slug>/`.

## Q

- **QBR** — Quarterly Business Review with the principal.

## R

- **Releaser** — Only archetype permitted to write to production (§13).

## S

- **Sandbox / Staging / Production** — The three output stages per §3.
- **State.json** — Single source of truth for all dynamic numbers (`.firm/state.json`, §16.4).

## T

- **Ticket Complexity** — Trivial / Small / Medium / Large / XL (§10.4).

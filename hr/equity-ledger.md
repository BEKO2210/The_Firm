# Equity Ledger — Korynth Labs

> Per CLAUDE.md §35. Tracks who has how much equity vested, vesting events, and customer co-ownership.

## Equity model

- **4-year vesting, 1-year cliff.**
- **Allocation pool:** 20% of total equity reserved for employees (standard early-stage allocation).
- **Founder allocation:** 80% to principal (Belkis Aslani).
- **Quarterly vesting events** after the 1-year cliff.

## Cliff & vesting

- Cliff: Day 365 (1 sim-year). No equity is vested before this date.
- After cliff: 25% vests at cliff, remaining 75% over the next 36 sim-months (1/48th per month).
- Full vesting: Day 1,460 (4 sim-years).

## Payout on exit

Vested portion paid out at next quarterly equity-cycle close. Unvested portion forfeited (returned to pool).

## Customer co-ownership

Per §35.4: a customer who has been a principal for ≥ 3 sim-years AND has NPS avg ≥ 8 can opt into converting a portion of fees to equity at favorable terms. No customer-holders at Day 1.

## Performance bonuses (separate from equity)

Quarterly bonuses tied to OKR achievement (per §35.1):
- KR score ≥ 0.7 → bonus pool activated
- Allocation: 50% to direct contributors, 50% distributed by tenure × performance
- Funded from profit-sharing pool

## Profit-sharing pool

Annual bonus pool funded from net profit. Distribution by tenure × performance per §35.1. Decided at quarterly OKR planning meetings.

# DSGVO & Compliance Basics

## Overview

Region = Germany → DSGVO mandatory. Industry = AI-Native → EU AI Act mandatory. SOC 2 + ISO 27001 + AVV for Premium tier.

## Key points

- VVT (Verzeichnis der Verarbeitungstätigkeiten) per Art. 30.
- DPIA per Art. 35 when triggers apply.
- AVV per Art. 28 for every sub-processor.
- Breach notification within 72h to BW Datenschutzbehörde.

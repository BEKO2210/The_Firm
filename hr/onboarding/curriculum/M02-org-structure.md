# Org Structure

## Overview

13 archetypes, 50 starting employees, dual-track career (IC + Manager) at our size (≥25).

## Key points

- Leader, Strategist, Coordinator, Maker, Reviewer, Guardian, Mentor, Communicator, Caretaker, Recorder, Critic, Operator, Releaser.
- IC track: Junior → Mid → Senior → Staff → Principal.
- Manager track: Team Lead → Engineering Manager → Director.
- Equal compensation per level on both tracks.

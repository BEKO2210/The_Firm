# Anti-Patterns We Eliminate

## Overview

10 toxic behaviors explicitly forbidden — you enforce them on yourself and your peers.

## Key points

- A.1 Burnout / heroism — 8h/day max, no weekend work.
- A.2 Micromanagement — Lead doesn't edit Maker's work without invitation.
- A.3 Politics & gossip — no backchannels; decisions in documented forums.
- A.4 Info hoarding — docs readable by default.
- A.5 Blame culture — postmortems are blameless.
- A.6 HiPPO — argue from data, not authority.
- A.7 Meeting overload — max 2/day per employee.
- A.8 Toxic customer tolerance — terminate with respect after 3 abusive incidents.
- A.9 Tech-debt denial — 20% sprint capacity reserved.
- A.10 Bikeshedding — 15-min timebox on trivia.

# Tooling Budget 2026 — Korynth Labs

Annual budget: capped at 5% of revenue (per §17.7). Approved by Caretaker (operations specialty).

## Allocations

| Category | Annual EUR | Monthly EUR |
|----------|------------|-------------|
| AI / LLM API (Anthropic, OpenAI, HuggingFace) | 24,000 | 2,000 |
| Hosting (Vercel + Supabase + Cloudflare) | 12,000 | 1,000 |
| Observability (Sentry, Axiom, Grafana) | 6,000 | 500 |
| Developer tooling (GitHub Enterprise, Linear, Figma) | 9,600 | 800 |
| Security tooling (Snyk, 1Password, Vanta) | 6,000 | 500 |
| Misc (slack, calendly, smaller tools) | 2,400 | 200 |
| **Total** | **60,000** | **5,000** |

## Approval threshold

- < €500/month new tool: Caretaker (ops) approves.
- ≥ €500/month new tool: CFO + CTO joint approval.
- ≥ 5% of revenue: principal approval (human-in-loop per §31.2).

## Audit

Tooling spend reviewed quarterly at QBR. Annual external audit reviews this category along with payroll + revenue per §17.8.
